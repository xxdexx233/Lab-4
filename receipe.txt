Ingredients (2 servings)
2 tomatoes
3 eggs
1 tsp salt
2 tbsp cooking oil
A little scallion (optional)
Steps
Cut tomatoes into chunks, beat eggs with a pinch of salt.
Heat oil in a pan, fry eggs until set, take out.
Sauté tomatoes in the same pan until soft and juicy.
Put eggs back, mix well, add salt to taste.
Sprinkle scallion (if using) and serve.
